How we share stories

Every voice is credited. Nothing is invented.

  • We republish a story only when it has an open licence or written permission, and we credit the author and original publisher.
  • Stories without that permission are linked to the original website. We do not copy their text or photographs.
  • We do not create stories, people, photographs, quotes or interviews.
  • Content notes tell you about difficult topics before you start reading.
Stories are not submitted or published automatically. There is no public story-submission form. A personal story shared with MAAM may appear here only after informed consent, privacy and safety review, and manual approval for publication. Please do not send private health details through this website.

If a story brings up difficult feelings, consider talking with someone you trust. You can also find organisations in the MAAM Support Directory.
