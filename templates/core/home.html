The meaning behind our name

Speaking up matters. So does making space to listen.

Mwana Asingachemi Anofira Mumbereko “The baby that does not cry will die in the cradle.”

This Shona proverb can remind us that expressing a need may help someone be noticed. It is not a reason to blame anyone who finds speaking difficult. Communities share responsibility for listening with compassion and helping people find credible information and appropriate support.
