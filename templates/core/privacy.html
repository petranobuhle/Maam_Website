Privacy

Privacy notice

Clear information about the details you choose to share with MAAM through this website.

Under development: MAAM is not yet publicly launched. This notice describes the current prototype and must be reviewed and finalized before public launch. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Who we are and why this website exists

MAAM (Mwana Asingachemi Anofira Mumbereko), also known as Beyond the Silence, is a youth-led mental health awareness and advocacy initiative. The website shares educational information, supports safer conversations and aims to help people find appropriate support. It is not a counselling, diagnostic, treatment or emergency service. Our initial community focus is young people in urban Zimbabwe, while recognizing that African communities have diverse experiences.

Information you may choose to provide

The website does not currently have a general contact form. If you email MAAM, the email address and any information in your message will be received through the email service used by the sender and MAAM's recipient account.

  • Volunteer form: your name, email, country, area of interest, availability, a short motivation and whether you consent to being contacted. These details are requested to review your volunteer interest and, where you agree, respond to you.
  • Partnership form: your organisation name, contact name, email, country, partnership type, message and whether you consent to being contacted. These details are requested to understand and respond to a partnership enquiry.
  • Survey, if one is active: confirmation of consent, a broad age band and answers to the survey questions. The survey is designed not to ask for your name or contact details. Written answers can still identify you if you include identifying details. There is no active public survey at this time.
  • Technical information: the website's hosting provider may process technical request or server-log information. The provider, information collected and log-retention arrangements have not yet been confirmed for production.

What not to send

Do not include mental health histories, diagnoses, medical information, passwords, financial details, or identifying information about another person in a form, survey answer or email. Do not use the website or its forms to report an emergency or request urgent help; submissions are not monitored as an emergency channel.

How submissions are handled

Volunteer and partnership forms save submitted details in the website's database. Those records can be viewed through the login-protected Django administration by accounts granted relevant permissions. The prototype does not have a published staff access policy, formal handling procedure or defined retention period. No automatic deletion process is configured, so records may remain until someone with appropriate access removes them.

Survey answers are stored as survey records. Public survey reporting is designed to show aggregate preset-choice results only: written responses are excluded, and small groups are suppressed. Do not put identifying information in a written answer, because authorized administrators may be able to view submitted records.

MAAM has not yet established a formal process or response timeframe for privacy requests. Before public launch, access, correction and deletion requests, retention periods, hosting logs and staff access arrangements need to be reviewed and documented.

Ask a privacy question or request action

You may email mahwadupetra@gmail.com to ask a privacy question or request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted. To help locate a record, include only the form type and approximate submission date, and the email address you used if applicable. Do not send sensitive health details. Requests will need to be reviewed; a response time or outcome is not guaranteed under a formal process at this stage.
