How MAAM approaches surveys

Participation is voluntary and requires consent. The current survey design is for adults aged 18–29 and asks for an age band rather than a date of birth.

MAAM is an awareness initiative, not a clinical research or diagnosis service. Survey questions and public reporting should be reviewed before a survey is launched.

Privacy protections

  • Written answers are excluded from public reports.
  • Public answer categories come only from preset survey choices.
  • Counts below five are suppressed.
  • When fewer than five responses exist, results are not shown.
